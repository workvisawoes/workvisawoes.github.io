Hello and welcome to Work Visa Woes! Thank you so much for taking the time to read plain text on a website! I really hope this experience is refreshing for you. It certainly is refreshing for me to type this into a blank screen and read it back without anyone trying to sell me perfectly cuboidal black bars of soap or hurtle me down the manosphere blackhole (again).

For you dear reader, whether you are my friend who I am subtly coercing into reading this for my own validation, or a stranger on the internet - I just hope you find some tidbit of information or perspective here that makes you a slightly more informed person or at least makes your day a little happier, or lighter or less unbearable.

This blog is simply my small vanity project for yelling things into the digital void. In the process, I want to simply clarify my own thoughts and experiences, being a working man on a Visa in the currently United States of America (2026 edition). Some disclaimers to start:

1. Yes, this blog will get political. Everything is, and has always been, political.

2. No, this blog is not JUST about working, visas and politics - I want to write freely and impulsively. There will be posts about food and fashion and VFX as well as maybe decolonization. Who knows!

3. I solemnly swear that I will not use any AI or LLMs to write the content of this blog.

I also thought it was funny that the default header in this blog template is always some stock photo of an immaculate desk - so in start contrast, I present a view of mine from an old self-aggrandizing timelapse video of me solving a rubik's cube. Anyway, please enjoy! I'm figuring this out as we go, but if you're on a mailing list or something, I would definitely love to hear your thoughts!

With maximum love,
RS
